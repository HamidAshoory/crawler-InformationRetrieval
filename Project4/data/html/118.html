Department of Electrical Engineering

The Department of Electrical Engineering at the Ferdowsi University of Mashhad is one the centers of excellence in electrical engineering in Iran. The three electrical engineering degree programs (B.S., Masters, and Ph.D.) follow the general aims of the Iranian higher education system. Benefiting from distinguished and outstanding human resources, the Department of Electrical Engineering provides professional, dynamic, and research-oriented graduates who have entrepreneurship capabilities to fulfill the society’s needs in Science and Technology.  More
