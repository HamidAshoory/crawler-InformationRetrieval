Signing of an executive agreement between the faculties of Ferdowsi University of Mashhad and Al-Iraqia University of Baghdad

29 Oct 2025
In continuation of the process of expanding scientific and international cooperation and in line with the implementation of the Memorandum of Understanding between Ferdowsi University of Mashhad (FUM) and Al-Iraqia University of Baghdad, executive contracts were signed between several faculties of the two universities. During this meeting, which was held on Wednesday, September 17, 2025, in the presence of Prof. Javan Jafari, President of Ferdowsi University of Mashhad, the accompanying delegation and senior officials of Al-Iraqia University, the faculties of Science, Engineering, Administrative and Economic Sciences, Law and Political Sciences, and Literature and Humanities of FUM signed specific executive contracts with their corresponding faculties at Al-Iraqia University.
