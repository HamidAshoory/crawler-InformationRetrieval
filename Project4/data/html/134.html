Department of Physics

The Department of Physics in the Faculty of Science at the Ferdowsi University of Mashhad was founded in September 1962, as a joint 3-year program in Mathematics and Physics. Later on, it was separated from the Department of Mathematics as an independent 4-year program in 1965.

The Department of Physics is dedicated to educate undergraduate and graduate students, and to convey the concepts and logic of the discipline to broader scientific and industrial communities. The students admitted to this program can study in either one of the following academic areas of specialization: Astronomy and astrophysics, Nuclear Physics, Elementary Particle Physics, Condensed matter physics and Geophysics.

Currently, the department admits students at undergraduate, graduate, and postgraduate levels. Bachelor’s students can enroll in the undergraduate program of physics. However, graduate and postgraduate students choose one of the following programs.

  • M.Sc. Program
    • Elementary Particle Physics, since 1970
    • Nuclear Physics, since 1973
    • Geophysics, since 1973
    • Astronomy and Astrophysics, since 1974
    • Condensed Matter, since 1974
    • Atomic and Molecular Physics, since 2011
    • Fundamental Physics-Quantum Information, since 2013
  • Ph.D. Program
    • Elementary Particle Physics, since 1993
    • Nuclear Physics, since 1993
    • Condensed Matter Physics, since 1993
    • Astronomy and Astrophysics, since 1996
    • String Theory-Oriented Particle Physics, since 2011
    • Fundamental Physics-Quantum Information, since 2013 More
