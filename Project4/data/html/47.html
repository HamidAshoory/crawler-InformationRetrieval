Prof. Abdolreza Javan Jafari Bojnordi 

Prof. Abdolreza Javan Jafari Bojnordi 

Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) in Criminal Sociology

Email: javan-j(at) um (dot) ac (dot) ir

website:  https://prof.um.ac.ir/javan-j
