Faculty of Engineering

  

Established in 1976, the Faculty of Engineering turned out to be one of the centers of excellence in the East of Iran. The Faculty of Engineering aims at cultivating technical talents in youths, developing specialized human resources, carrying out industrial projects, and turning Iran into an independent country in industry and science. Comprising of seven fields of study and 68 sub-fields, as well as benefiting from a large number of laboratories, the Faculty of Engineering offers a vast range of degree programs at the B.S., M.S., and Ph.D. levels which make it one of the biggest facultys at the Ferdowsi University of Mashhad.

The four research centers in the Faculty of Engineering include Research Center for Energy Conservation and Air Pollution Control, Research Center for Water Sources and Environment, Research Center for Computer and Electronics, and Research Center for Manufacturing Engineering and Automotive Services.More
