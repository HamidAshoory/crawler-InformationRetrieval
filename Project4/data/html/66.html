Study at FUM

A vast range of majors and courses are available at the Ferdowsi University of Mashhad. You can apply for either a Bachelor, Masters or Ph.D. degree in most fields. All educational programs are subject based. During your studies you can enjoy Persian Language, and high-quality academics and an attractive environment. All facilities at the FUM including dormitories, clubs, sports and recreational facilities, high-speed Internet access, well-equipped libraries and quiet-reading areas have distinguished the FUM as one of the best academic environments in Iran for students. These are some reasons for many Iranian and International students to choose the Ferdowsi University of Mashhad  for their higher education studies.
